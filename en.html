About YUS

Beauty isn't decoration — it's a tool you can use every day.

I spent ten years in commercial marketing, helping brands find their voice — while it took me far longer to learn how to listen to my own. What eventually brought me back was never a harder push, but small things: a stroke of colour, an honest conversation, a beautiful sheet of paper.

That's how YUS began: turning art and awareness into tools you can reach for in everyday life. This isn't about becoming a better person — it's about reminding you that you already are enough.

(a note on the side)

It really did take a long time. Along the way I tried squeezing myself back into the shape I thought I was supposed to be — and found I couldn't. Looking back now, those moments of not fitting were exactly where YUS began. If you're stuck somewhere you can't quite name — I understand. I've been there too. And I later found that lightness and ease can, in fact, come easily.

Founder · S

A graduate of Taipei National University of the Arts, with over a decade in brand marketing and mindfulness practice, certified as a mindfulness trainer in 2019. Now working in brand consulting, mindful breathing, and art healing — bringing the most rational mind to the most emotional conversations.

NEW MOON

Mindful Breathing

30 min · NT$ 680 · online

An MBSR body scan — from the top of your head downward, inch by inch, back into your body. Then two or three breathing practices. Different ones for different moments: one for falling asleep, one for before a meeting, one for 3am when your mind won't stop.

You leave with the steps written down and a recording — breathing tools you can keep practising at home.

Mindful / Art Therapy Session

1-on-1 · 60 min · NT$ 1,680 · online

Bring something specific if you like — work, a relationship, the decision you've been stuck on. Or bring nothing at all, and just practise breathing and remember what it feels like to make something.

Breathing first, then talking or drawing, then we look at what came out together. You really don't need to know how to draw. How good it looks was never the point.
