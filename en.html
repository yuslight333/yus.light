About YUS

Beauty isn’t decoration —
it’s a tool you can use every day.

I spent ten years in commercial marketing helping brands find their voice, and far longer learning to listen to my own. What brought me back was never a harder push. It was small things: a stroke of colour, an honest conversation, a beautiful sheet of paper.

That’s how YUS began: turning art and awareness into tools you can reach for in everyday life. I’m not trying to make you a better person. I just want to help you remember you’re already enough.

Founder · S

A graduate of Taipei National University of the Arts, with over a decade in brand marketing and mindfulness practice, certified as a mindfulness trainer in 2019.

Now working across brand consulting, mindful breathing and art facilitation, bringing the most rational mind to the most emotional conversations.
