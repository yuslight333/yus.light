About YUS

Beauty isn't decoration — it's a tool you can use every day.

I spent ten years in commercial marketing, helping brands find their voice — while it took me far longer to learn how to listen to my own. What eventually brought me back was never a harder push, but small things: a stroke of colour, an honest conversation, a beautiful sheet of paper.

That's how YUS began: turning art and awareness into tools you can reach for in everyday life. This isn't about becoming a better person — it's about reminding you that you already are enough.

(a note on the side)

It really did take a long time. Along the way I tried squeezing myself back into the shape I thought I was supposed to be — and found I couldn't. Looking back now, those moments of not fitting were exactly where YUS began. If you're stuck somewhere you can't quite name — I understand. I've been there too. And I later found that lightness and ease can, in fact, come easily.

Founder · S

A graduate of Taipei National University of the Arts, with over a decade in brand marketing and mindfulness practice, certified as a mindfulness trainer in 2019. Now working in brand consulting, mindful breathing, and creative wellbeing — bringing the most rational mind to the most emotional conversations.

Mindful Art Session · 1-on-1

One-on-one. Starting with a quiet conversation — or just a pen — combining mindful breathing, artistic awareness, and subconscious exploration to help you hear your own voice. Open to any topic — career, relationships, personal growth. Or come with nothing in mind at all, and simply practice breathing and remember what it feels like to create. (There's no schedule to keep. Mindful Art Workshop

Small groups. Using brush and colour to put feelings on paper that words can't quite reach. (No drawing skill required — how "good" it looks was never the point.) Held occasionally across Asia — past workshops in Shanghai, Kuala Lumpur, and Bali — and available for private/corporate bookings.
